# Financial Viability Invariants

## Invariants
1. **Positive Net Present Value**: The 36-month Discounted Cash Flow model must yield $\text{NPV} > 0$ at an annual hurdle rate of $8.5\%$.
2. **Maximum Payback Period**: Discounted payback period $T_{\text{payback}}$ must not exceed 18 months under baseline volume projections.
3. **Minimum ROI Threshold**: 3-year projected Return on Investment ($\text{ROI}$) must exceed $25.0\%$.
4. **Unit Economic Feasibility**: Incremental inference and deliberation cost must not exceed $30\%$ of the downstream economic value delivered per transaction/token.
